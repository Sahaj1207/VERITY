# VERITY Frontend Scaffolding & Roadmap

**VERITY — Financial Truth, Reconstructed**  
*AI Finance Controller Dashboard*

---

## Overview

The VERITY frontend will provide Indian SMB owners, accountants, and freelancers with an intuitive, visually stunning Financial Truth Dashboard to view, inspect, and resolve reconciliation results.

## Roadmap (Day 2 / Day 3 Scope)

1. **Reconciliation Overview Feed**:
   - Status filters: `CONFIRMED`, `PARTIAL`, `DUPLICATE`, `CONTRADICTED`, `UNVERIFIABLE`, `AMBIGUOUS`.
   - Real-time confidence score indicators and summary metrics.

2. **Multimodal Evidence Inspector**:
   - Side-by-side view comparing raw WhatsApp chat exports, invoice PDFs, payment screenshots, and bank statement CSV rows.
   - Text highlight for extracted claims and confidence meters.

3. **Interactive Lineage & Provenance Graph**:
   - Visual Directed Acyclic Graph (DAG) demonstrating the path from raw evidence artifact to verified conclusion.
   - Clickable nodes with SHA-256 tamper verification.

4. **Exception & Discrepancy Action Center**:
   - Quick one-click resolution workflows for `AMBIGUOUS` invoice pairings and `UNVERIFIABLE` cash claims.
